not a promise.

Accuracy depends on the MacBook, desk, room, and laptop position. Every setup runs its own guided 60-tap evaluation, with results saved to that desk profile.

FAQ

What can a tap do?

Play a sound, copy or speak text, open a website, run a Shortcut, open an app, file, or folder, execute a shell command, or take a screenshot.

Does Holo work on every desk?

No universal accuracy claim is made. Stable, rigid surfaces are the best candidates, and moving the MacBook changes the acoustic path. Use Holo’s 60-tap evaluation to measure your setup.

Does it record me?

Raw audio is held only long enough to analyze a short tap window, then discarded by default. Optional debug recordings must be enabled explicitly and stay local.

What does Holo require?

macOS 14 or later on a MacBook, using its built-in microphone. Holo is functional experimental software, so expect to calibrate and test it on your own desk.
