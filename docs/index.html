Holo

Tap your desk.
Your Mac responds.

A native macOS utility that turns the space around your MacBook into four acoustic tap zones.

Download latest release View on GitHub ↗

Four zones.
Your actions.

Left. Right. Front. Rear.

How it works

  1. 01

    Calibrate. Tap each zone ten times.

  2. 02

    Assign. Pick one action per zone.

  3. 03

    Tap. Your Mac runs it.

Private by default.

  • Local. Tap processing stays on your Mac.
  • Sandboxed. No audio or profiles are uploaded.
  • Discarded. Raw audio is discarded by default.

Good to know.

  • Calibrate and run the 60-tap test on your own desk.
  • Accuracy changes with the desk, room, and laptop position.
  • Requires macOS 14+ and a MacBook’s built-in microphone.
